The situation

Where the account started.

A ferry booking platform selling routes worldwide, in 24 languages, on four ad platforms. The year's work was to grow booking value in the core markets and open new ones.

The work

What was done.

  • +Scaled dedicated Google Ads campaigns for five core language markets to 2.2 times the budget.
  • +Cut spend on weaker generic and route Search by 35% and kept the queries that earn their cost.
  • +Took Performance Max into four new markets, growing them from €95K to €547K of spend at a return of 6x to 7x.
  • +Launched 245 new Search campaigns, 179 of them in new regions.
  • +Scaled Microsoft Ads by 56%, with budgets paced daily, weekly and monthly against return targets.
  • +Grew Apple Search Ads budget by 11% and directed it to the keywords that convert to installs.
  • +Held Meta spend flat and focused it on sales campaigns.
