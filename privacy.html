Privacy

This page explains what happens to the details you send through this website.

What is collected

When you request an audit, you send your name, work email, website and approximate monthly ad spend by email. Nothing is collected unless you choose to send it.

How it is used

Your details are used only to reply to your request and to prepare the audit you asked for. They are not sold or shared with third parties.

Cookies and tracking

No optional cookies are set unless you choose Accept in the cookie banner. If you accept, cookies may be used to measure visits to the site. If you decline, none are set. Your choice is saved in your browser, and you can change it by clearing this site's data. Fonts are served from this website itself.

Newsletter

If you subscribe to the newsletter, your email address is used only to send it. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details you have sent at any time by writing to info@futurebrainmarketing.com.
